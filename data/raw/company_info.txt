TechNova AI

TechNova AI is a fictional software company founded in 2021.
The company develops artificial intelligence and cloud computing solutions.

Products:
1. NovaAssist - an AI-powered customer support platform.
2. NovaSearch - a semantic enterprise search system.
3. NovaVision - a computer vision API.

Office:
TechNova AI headquarters is located in Austin, Texas.

Customer Support:
Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central Time.
Premium customers receive 24/7 support.

Refund Policy:
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
Refund requests must include the customer's order number.

Security:
TechNova AI encrypts customer data both in transit and at rest.
Customer passwords are hashed and are never stored as plain text.

Technology:
TechNova AI uses Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud infrastructure.
The AI platform uses vector search and large language models for semantic search and question answering.
